A one-day newspaper front page that sums up **Thursday 26 November 2026**'s
world news in a single image — drawn in code (`poster.html`, 1024×1024) and
rendered once to `entry.png`.

## The five stories

1. **Wall Street plunges** — the Nasdaq fell about 4% and Nvidia slid 8.7%
   on the last session before Thanksgiving, as the AI sell-off met a fresh
   round of Trump tariffs on 14 trading partners (a further 20% on China).
   The S&P 500 and Nasdaq are on course for their worst November since 2008.
2. **Sudan** — RSF drone strikes killed 19 civilians in Damazin, Blue Nile
   state (including six women, 20 wounded, per Emergency Lawyers), and hit
   the dam and power facilities at Roseires.
3. **Space** — Pakistan's first astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, flew to
   China's Tiangong station aboard Shenzhou-24 — the first foreign astronaut
   on a Chinese crewed mission.
4. **Diplomacy** — North Korea ordered Switzerland to close its newly opened
   diplomatic office in Pyongyang and expelled envoy Sylvain Astier within
   48 hours, in a row over sanctions on a children's camp.
5. **Sport** — World chess champion D Gukesh opened his title defence in
   Geneva with a 38-move draw against Javokhir Sindarov, while 15-year-old
   Vaibhav Sooryavanshi smashed 204 off 53 balls (28 sixes) against the UAE
   — the fastest List A double century ever.

Also today: Colombia police freed 20 hostages after a 17-hour supermarket
siege in Cartagena; Bangladesh raised garment workers' minimum wage by 36%;
India pledged a ₹15,000-crore package for Nepal; a 5.6-magnitude quake shook
Nepal's Koshi province; six died in a plane crash off the Mississippi coast;
India marked Constitution Day.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png` in the
grader). Node 22, standard library only, no network, deterministic.
