LEAD STORY • WALL STREET

The AI boom hits the wall

The Nasdaq plunged about 4% and Nvidia slid 8.7% on Wednesday, the last session before Thanksgiving, as doubts over the debt-fuelled AI boom met a fresh round of US tariffs on 14 trading partners — a further 20% on China.

WAR IN SUDAN

Drones kill 19 in Blue Nile

RSF drones struck three residential areas of Damazin, killing 19 civilians — six of them women — and wounding 20, rights group Emergency Lawyers said. Drones also hit the dam and power plants at Roseires.

SPACE

Pakistan reaches orbit

Muhammad Zeeshan Ali became the first Pakistani in space — and the first foreign astronaut aboard China’s Tiangong station — as Shenzhou-24 docked after launching from Jiuquan on Wednesday.

DIPLOMACY

Pyongyang shuts Swiss door

North Korea expelled envoy Sylvain Astier and ordered Switzerland to close its newly opened Pyongyang office within 48 hours, in a row over sanctions on a children’s camp.

SPORT

Chess: Gukesh holds Sindarov — and a 15-year-old smashes 204 off 53

World champion D Gukesh opened his title defence in Geneva with a 38-move draw against Javokhir Sindarov. In Doha, 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi hit 204 off 53 balls — 28 sixes — the fastest List A double century ever, as India A chased 250 in 14.2 overs.
